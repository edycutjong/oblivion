The Problem

Cross-chain swaps are public, risky, and extractable.

  • 🔓 Trade intent leaks — Cross-chain swaps broadcast intent. Searchers front-run before settlement.
  • 🌉 Bridge vulnerabilities — $2.5B+ lost to bridge exploits since 2021. Bridges are the weakest link.
  • 🐌 Slow finality — Cross-chain settlement takes minutes. Users wait. Capital is locked.

The Solution

Encrypted intent + bridgeless MPC settlement. Your swap is invisible until it's done.

🔒

Encrypted Intent

Encrypt SDK wraps swap intent. No one sees what you're trading until execution.

⚛️

Atomic Settlement

Ika MPC enables bridgeless cross-chain execution. Both sides settle or neither does.

🛡️

MEV-Proof

Encrypted state prevents front-running. Your swap price is the real price.
